**Publication** — Rodents love to gnaw… which can be cute or annoying (e.g., when they decided to destroy my car wiring). But our new collaborative study published in <a href="https://www.cell.com/neuron/fulltext/S0896-6273(26)00046-2">Neuron</a> uncovers why. Led by Dr. Xinyu Su and Bo Duan Lab, we discovered that incisor tactile input is relayed by a sensorimotor-motivational circuit to drive gnawing. Congratulations to all the authors! Featured in <a href="https://news.umich.edu/gnaw-y-by-nature-u-m-researchers-discover-neural-circuit-that-rewards-gnawing-behavior-in-rodents/">UMich News</a> and <a href="https://www.science.org/content/article/why-do-rodents-gnaw-because-it-feels-good">Science</a>.
